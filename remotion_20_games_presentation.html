What happened?

What changed?

  • Success %, response latency
  • Sequencing and omission errors
  • Trajectory estimates and variability

14 / Clinical safeguards

Safe, adaptable, therapist-led.

Always individualize eligibility. These precautions are general design guardrails, not a substitute for clinical screening or device-specific risk management.

!
Medical / movement restrictions

Stop for new symptoms, uncontrolled pain or prohibited movements; respect load and range restrictions.

↔
Fall and postural risk

Provide seated options and clinical guarding for standing or stepping tasks.

◌
VR discomfort

Stop with dizziness, nausea or visual disturbance. Prefer stable scenes and easy exits.

◒
Visual & sensory accessibility

No rapid flashes, color-only signals or unnecessary auditory/visual overload.

⌁
Sensor uncertainty

Label calibration and confidence; tracking loss should pause, never count as success.

◇
Measurement transparency

Do not represent camera estimates or game scores as validated clinical tests.

Regulatory classification, clinical evidence, data protection and accessibility requirements need appropriate review before deploying games as medical features.
